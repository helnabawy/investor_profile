# Global competitive landscape: agent website builders and personal-brand site products (as of November 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-11. Verification legend used throughout:
- **[P]** = read directly on the vendor's own page on 2026-11-11 (primary).
- **[T]** = read directly on a third-party page on 2026-11-11 (page fetched in full).
- **[S]** = taken from a search-result summary only; the underlying page was not opened (several vendor sites returned HTTP 403 to automated fetch). Treat as lower confidence.
- **Bias warning:** a large share of what ranks for "X pricing 2026" is published by Luxury Presence (a competitor of every vendor it writes about), by WPResidence (a WordPress theme vendor), or by anonymous `*.pages.dev` affiliate/AI-content sites. These are flagged where used.

## 1. Who are the main agent-website vendors and what do they charge (setup fee + monthly)?

### Takeaway
The market splits into three price bands: (a) premium done-for-you brand sites with a $800-$6,500+ setup fee and $150-$500+/month on a 12-month term (Luxury Presence, AgentFire, Agent Image, Easy Agent Pro); (b) IDX + CRM + lead-gen "all-in-one" platforms at roughly $300-$1,800/month, mostly quote-only (Real Geeks, Sierra Interactive, Lofty, BoldTrail/kvCORE, Ylopo); (c) DIY at $16-$129/month (Placester, Wix, Squarespace, WordPress themes at $69-$79 one-time). Almost all purpose-built vendors assume a US MLS/IDX feed as the core of the product.

### Inferences
- A premium personal-brand site for one agent costs about $2,800-$6,000+ in year one at AgentFire (setup $800 + 12 x $165 = $2,780 minimum) and more at Luxury Presence ($2,500-$3,500 setup plus an unpublished monthly; using the 2023 $250/month figure gives at least $5,500).
- Quote-only pricing is the norm at the top of the market (Luxury Presence, Agent Image, Lofty, BoldTrail). Published self-serve pricing is itself a point of differentiation.
- The cheap end (Wix, Squarespace, WP themes, Placester Essential) sells a site with no meaningful AI and no real-estate-specific lead handling beyond forms.
- For a non-US market such as Dubai, the IDX/MLS component that justifies much of the US price is irrelevant, so the comparable value is the design, hosting, lead capture and AI layers only.

### Gaps
- Luxury Presence's actual monthly prices per plan in 2026 could not be verified; the vendor pages returned 403 and no third party publishes a current figure.
- Agent Image pricing is unverified beyond one competitor-authored article.
- Placester, Real Geeks, Sierra Interactive and BoldTrail prices were not confirmed on vendor pages (403/404 or quote-only).
- Official 2026 list prices for Wix, Squarespace and Webflow plans were not read on vendor pages; figures above are relayed by third parties and are inconsistent.
- No UK/EU/AU-specific agent-site vendors were researched (the query set surfaced only US-centric vendors); this is an open gap for the "global" framing.
- Ylopo website pricing was only found bundled with its ad/AI product (see section 2).

## 2. Which vendors ship an AI chatbot/assistant grounded in the agent's own listings on the agent's website, and how is it priced?

### Takeaway
Most "AI" in this category is outbound lead nurture by SMS, email and voice after a form fill, sold as an add-on or bundled into $400-$1,000+/month platforms; a true on-site chat widget grounded in the agent's own inventory and notes is rare, and where listing grounding exists it comes through a US IDX/MLS feed rather than agent-curated content. No vendor found pairs that capability with a WhatsApp handoff.

- **Luxury Presence:** AI Lead Nurture launched 21 February 2024; the assistant ("Alex" by default) engages after a website contact form, home-search sign-up or landing-page submission, sends the first SMS within minutes, qualifies, and follows up for up to a year. Vendor claims over 50% lead response rate and over 14% warm handoff rate across an average 25 touchpoints. It was later repackaged as the "AI Lead Nurture Specialist" inside an "AI Marketing Specialist Team" that launched to customers 4 August 2025, and sits in the Leads Pro plan. ### Inferences
- The incumbents' AI answers the question "how do I chase the lead after the form fill", not "how does the site itself answer a buyer's question about this unit". EstateSite's concierge sits in the second, less crowded slot.
- Where incumbents ground AI in inventory, the grounding source is an IDX/MLS feed. An agent-curated knowledge base (own projects, FAQs, notes) is a different design that suits markets without MLS, such as Dubai off-plan.
- AI is consistently monetised as an upsell on top of a $300+/month base, or by usage (Structurely per-minute/per-message, Lofty per-lead blocks). An AI concierge included in a low flat price would be unusual.
- Structurely's move to usage-priced voice/SMS for sales teams suggests the standalone "AI ISA for solo agents" niche has moved upmarket, leaving the solo-agent end less served.

### Gaps
- Could not verify whether Luxury Presence, Lofty or Sierra have an on-site, visitor-facing chat widget that answers from listings in late 2026; vendor product pages were not accessible or not explicit.
- Lofty AI pricing is unresolved (three conflicting figures; official page says "Request Pricing").
- Roof AI's current product status and pricing were not confirmed on a vendor page.
- No vendor was found offering WhatsApp handoff from an on-site real-estate concierge; absence in these searches is not proof of absence, particularly for MENA/India-focused tools that were out of scope here.
- Many chatbot price points come from anonymous `*.pages.dev` content sites and should not be quoted without re-verification.

## 4. What are the recurring complaints in reviews?

### Takeaway
Recurring themes are opaque quote-only pricing, setup fees plus 12-month or auto-renewing contracts, not owning the site (rebuild from scratch on exit), dependence on the vendor for edits, poor lead quality from managed ads, and slow support. Evidence here is thin and partly competitor-authored; no Reddit r/realtors threads surfaced in search.

### Cited Findings
- Luxury Presence ratings (October 2026): G2 4.5 from 72 reviews; Trustpilot 2.2 from 10 reviews; BBB 1.14 from 7 reviews. Small samples; relayed by a competitor. [T] — [Espo.ai decision guide](https://espo.ai/resources/luxury-presence-decision-guide)

## 5. Is there a low-price, self-serve tier gap (under $50/month with AI chat included)?

### Takeaway
Yes, on the evidence gathered: no vendor found sells a real-estate-specific personal site with a listing-grounded AI chat for under $50/month. Sub-$50 options are either a generic site with no AI concierge, or a chatbot with no site and no listing knowledge.

### Cited Findings
- Under $50/month site options: Wix about $17-$29, Squarespace about $16-$29, iNCOM $44.95 (plus $250 setup), Placester Essential about $47 on annual billing ($59 monthly), B12 Website $49. None is described as including an AI chat grounded in the agent's listings. [T]/[S] — [The Close](https://theclose.com/best-real-estate-website-builders); [WPResidence](https://wpresidence.net/?p=8076); [PricingSaaS Placester](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/placester)
- Under $50/month chat options: Tidio from $29/month with no native listing data; AeroChat from $29/month for website, WhatsApp and Instagram capture, no site builder. [T]/[S] — [RealtyCopilot](https://realty-copilot.pages.dev/blog/real-estate-chatbot-for-agents/); [AeroChat](https://aerochat.ai/blog/best-ai-chatbot-for-real-estate-agents-in-the-us)
- PISCES, an AI site builder, has a $29/month launch tier covering 3 pages on a custom domain; lead capture is by quote forms and no chatbot is mentioned. [S], vendor page — [Marketing by Magnet / PISCES](https://marketingbymagnet.com/ai-website-builder-for-real-estate-agents)
- Real Estate 7 WordPress theme reportedly bundles an AI chatbot at $129/year, but requires self-managed WordPress hosting. [S], vendor page — [Contempo Themes](https://contempothemes.com/choosing-the-best-real-estate-website-builder-a-side-by-side-comparison/)
- The cheapest purpose-built platforms with any AI lead engagement start well above $50: AgentFire Plus $215/month (AI drips only), Easy Agent Pro $299/month for a non-AI chat bot, Sierra about $300-$360 base plus about $199 for Lead Engage, Real Geeks from $399, Ylopo $400-$1,000+. [P]/[T]/[S] — [AgentFire](https://agentfire.com/pricing/); [Easy Agent Pro](https://www.easyagentpro.com/pricing/); [CostBench Sierra](https://www.costbench.com/software/real-estate-crm/sierra-interactive/); [The Close](https://theclose.com/best-real-estate-website-builders)
- A custom-built listing chatbot is estimated at $2,000-$10,000 upfront plus $100-$300/month. [T], low-quality source — [RealtyCopilot](https://realty-copilot.pages.dev/blog/real-estate-chatbot-for-agents/)

### Inferences
- The do-it-yourself equivalent of EstateSite today is a stack: generic site ($16-$29) plus chat widget ($29+) plus manual work to teach the bot the inventory, with no real-estate page types. That is roughly $45-$60/month and several hours of setup for a weaker result, which frames a plausible price ceiling for a self-serve tier and a floor for perceived value.
- The gap exists partly because US vendors anchor price on IDX/MLS licensing, CRM and ad management. A product without those costs can sit lower, but also lacks the features US agents treat as table stakes, so the gap is most exploitable in non-MLS markets.
- Between about $60 and $165/month there are few premium-design options (Placester Plus/Premier, Espo at $200); a mid tier with strong design and AI concierge would face little direct competition on the evidence here.
- Risk: general-purpose builders (Wix, Squarespace, B12) are adding AI assistants quickly, and a listing-aware chat could arrive as a feature there; this was not confirmed either way in this research.

### Gaps
- Whether Wix or Squarespace now include a visitor-facing AI chat agent trained on site content in their sub-$30 plans (as of late 2026) was not verified; this is the most important unknown for the gap claim.
- The search for 2025-2026 AI-native entrants was shallow (Espo.ai, PISCES, AeroChat surfaced); a fuller scan of Product Hunt, app marketplaces and MENA/India vendors is needed before claiming the gap is unoccupied.
- No data on how many agents actually buy sub-$50 products versus premium ones, beyond ThemeForest sales counts.
